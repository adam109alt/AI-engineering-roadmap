# Qwen

What is qwen? Qwen is an open-source model from the open AI models from Alibaba cloud and it's currently have 8 models: Two use a mixture-of-experts (MoE) architecture (Qwen3-235B-A22B and Qwen3-30B-A3B), while six use a dense architecture (Qwen3-32B, Qwen3-14B, Qwen3-8B, Qwen3-4B, Qwen3-1.7B, and Qwen3-0.6B)
And Qwen one of the biggest models that support languages, Curenttly Qwen support (119 language), Qwen 3 trained roughly twice the data of Qwen 2.5
